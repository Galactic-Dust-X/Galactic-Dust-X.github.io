Privacy Policy – shortsdata

Last updated: November 2, 2026

What data we access

shortsdata uses Google OAuth with the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope only. This allows the app to create files (video clips and related data files) in your Google Drive and to access only those files. The app cannot read, list or modify any other files in your Drive.

How we use it

Data is used solely to upload and manage the clips the app generates for you. We do not use your data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not share it with third parties.

Storage and retention

Files are stored in your own Google Drive. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the machine running the app and are used only to perform uploads. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the files from your Drive whenever you like.

Google API Services User Data Policy

shortsdata's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
